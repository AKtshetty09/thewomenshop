About us

The Women Shop is a women's ethnic wear boutique from Ongole, Andhra Pradesh. We handpick anarkali sets, A-line kurtis, top & dupatta sets, kurta sets, sarees and dresses, and ship them all over India.

Our promise is simple: you'll get exactly what you see. Every photo is real, and every piece is quality checked before it is packed.

Join our family of followers on Instagram for new arrivals and real customer reviews.

How to order

  1. Choose your product and size, and tap Add to Bag or Buy now on WhatsApp.
  2. Your order details are sent to us on WhatsApp with an Order ID.
  3. We confirm availability and share payment details in the chat.
  4. Once payment is received, your order is packed and shipped.

Not sure about your size? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose.

Payment

  • Prepaid orders only. We do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD).
  • Payment details are shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.
  • Orders are dispatched only after payment is received.

Shipping & delivery

  • We ship all over India from Ongole, Andhra Pradesh.
  • Delivery usually takes after dispatch.
  • Shipping charges: . For more items, or heavy items, the charge depends on the parcel weight and we confirm it on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • Some styles are made to order after you order. We confirm their dispatch date on WhatsApp.
  • Delivery time may be longer during festivals, sales or for remote locations.
  • For any update on your order, message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID.

Damaged items: exchange

If your product arrives damaged, we will exchange it.

  • An unboxing / parcel opening video is required for every exchange request.
  • Please record the video from the moment you start opening the sealed parcel, without cuts or pauses, clearly showing the damage.
  • Send the video with your Order ID to us on WhatsApp.
  • Exchange requests without an unboxing video cannot be accepted.

Refunds

  • We do not offer refunds.
  • Damaged products are exchanged as described above.

Privacy

  • You don't need an account to shop. We don't ask for your details on this website.
  • Your bag, wishlist, recently viewed items and My Orders list are saved only on your own phone or browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • When you order, your name, phone number and delivery address are shared with us on WhatsApp. We use them only to process and deliver your order, and share your address only with the courier.
  • This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits anonymously with Umami (no cookies, no personal data) to see which products are popular. Fonts and product photos are loaded from Google and other image hosts, which may see your IP address, as with any website.
  • Questions about your data? Message us on WhatsApp.
